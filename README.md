Online play (beta)

Messages, bloodstains and wandering ghosts from other hunters, through The Hunter's Dream.
Sign-in, bells and summons through a shadNet server (shadPS4's public server by default).
New Online co-op page in the launcher, with a Test connection button.
How to go online:

Register an account at https://shadnet.shadps4.net
In the launcher, open Online co-op, tick Play online, and enter your account name (not your email) and password.
Press PLAY and choose online at the title screen. The Hunter's Dream welcome message means you are online.
Summoning between players is new and not confirmed yet. Tell us on Discord how it goes.
